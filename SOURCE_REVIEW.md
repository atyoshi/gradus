# Fux source review — 31 October 2026

Inspected the 33 pinned original score pages covering the 46 v2 disagreements and consulted the 1725 primary text. These are evidence-backed issue classifications, not an independent certification of every note in the complete corpus. No rule reads this ledger to exempt a figure ID. Unsettled inflections and simultaneous dissonances remain visible.

## Method and limits

The original 46 v2 disagreements are preserved with their note IDs and messages in [the evidence ledger](public/sources/fux-review.json). The 33 source pages were inspected as scans, and their surrounding passages checked against the [1725 primary text](https://www.digital.wienbibliothek.at/wbrobv/download/ftpack/plain/4118035). The source file is pinned by SHA-256: `1e1e37e726a445b7020e7194ea60be8b0d4ffe9e74d824aac416cb4d9563ec4b`. Original pitches, timings, rests and ties have not been rewritten.

The ledger records checker bugs, source-supported exceptions, transcription uncertainties and questions still requiring source review. No case has been labeled an intentional teaching error without evidence. A corrected source example is distinguished from an erroneous student attempt. The checker does not use figure IDs as exemptions. Current pass counts measure implemented checks, not independent musical certification.

## Result

Current assessment of all 123 examples: 106 pass, 12 needs-review, 5 violations. These totals include new contextual review states; they are not directly comparable to the v2 error-only pass count.

Source-supported corrections include both directions of the minor-sixth leap (p.60), the first-species repetition preference rather than a numeric prohibition (corrected fig.15), fuller cadential ties and resolutions (pp.95–96), dissonant preparation over a stationary bass (pp.106–107), the whole-bar rest in the suspension study (p.108), and the consonant-entry eighth-note ornament (pp.112–113). Structural parallels are differentiated by species and texture.

Unsettled inflections remain review findings rather than definitive errors or a clean pass. Simultaneous dissonances between moving voices, including parts of figures 134, 183, 186, 203 and 204, remain explicitly unresolved. The suspected clef issue and original erratum relevant to fig.55 remain editorial evidence.

## Reproduction

Run `node scripts/audit_sources.ts` to reassess the immutable scores while retaining the review observations. Run `npm test` for manually specified allowed/prohibited figures, legacy-score compatibility, and source-preservation checks. Parnasso was used to discover differences, never as the expected-result oracle.
